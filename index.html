Patrol Participation Thresholds

A troop's participation % is their patrol hours since deployment, divided by total unit patrol hours over that same period. Set the cutoffs used to flag status.

%
🔴 Red is anything below the Yellow cutoff. This is a raw share of total unit hours (not a fair-share ratio), so tune these two numbers to your own roster size — e.g. with 20 troops sharing patrols evenly, a fair share is roughly 5% each.

Day & Night Times
When "day" turns into "night" for the duty roster — used to pick which strength number and duration applies.
Administration/HQ on Sentry
Administration/HQ staff are always a last resort for Sentry — pulled in only once the regular troop pool is exhausted. By day that stays absolute. By night, cap how many Administration/HQ troops can appear on a single night's Sentry roster, so a manpower shortage doesn't fill the whole roster with them.
Drivers on Duty
Drivers mix into the same Sentry posts and Roaming slots as everyone else — set exactly how many drivers should be on each per day/night. Whichever drivers are picked still rotate serially and by fairness, same as everyone else, so it's never the same drivers every time.
